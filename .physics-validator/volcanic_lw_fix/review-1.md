- **B2: the “exact fractional-pressure-overlap” is not exact for this source.** The loader creates OD from extinction per geometric-height interval, `ext [1/km] × dz [km]`. [`external.py:813`](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/tools/legoesm/forcing/external.py:813) The remapper then assumes that OD is uniform in pressure. [`surface_utils.py:275`](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/tools/legoesm/forcing/surface_utils.py:275), [`surface_utils.py:318`](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/tools/legoesm/forcing/surface_utils.py:318) For a height-uniform extinction layer, its CDF is height-based (approximately log-pressure, not linear pressure). Thus it conserves total OD, but misallocates OD whenever a model interface cuts a 0.5-km source layer. Preserve/remap the altitude edges, or use an inverse \(p\to z\) mapping, before claiming exact vertical overlap.

- **B3: Planck weighting is a reasonable gray heuristic, not “the correct gray equivalent.”** [`external.py:121`](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/tools/legoesm/forcing/external.py:121) A scalar \(\tau\) applied at every g-point cannot reproduce the bandwise response: the appropriate first-order weights are the full spectral radiative kernel, including surface/cloud/gas transmission and local layer emission, not simply \(B_{\tilde\nu}(220\,K)\). Per-band support is not mandatory with the present gray API, but this needs a documented approximation and a band-resolved reference test, especially for eruption amplitudes.
