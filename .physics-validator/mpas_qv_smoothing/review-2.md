- **BLOCKER — the claimed separate moisture conservation path does not exist for MPAS.** The new operator conserves \(\sum A q_v\), not water mass; that is correctly documented in [operators_voronoi.py:1253](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/core/legoesm/core/operators_voronoi.py:1253). But the actual moisture metric is \(\sum A\,p_s\,d\sigma\,q_v/g\) ([conservation.py:766](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/core/legoesm/core/conservation.py:766)). `_run_mpas` goes directly from the model step to smoothing ([model_driver.py:6615](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/model_driver.py:6615)); it does not call `fix_moisture_hydrostatic`. That fixer is in the FV/per-step tail, and even there it runs before the smoother ([compiled_segments.py:1402](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/compiled_segments.py:1402), [compiled_segments.py:1410](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/compiled_segments.py:1410)).

  Thus, across a pressure gradient, equal-area mixing-ratio diffusion changes CWV: moving \(\delta q\) from cell 1 to 2 changes mass by \(A\,d\sigma\,(p_{s,2}-p_{s,1})\delta q/g\). The sign depends on the humidity–terrain correlation, so this is an uncorrected bias, not merely roundoff. It may be acceptable only as an explicitly non-conservative opt-in filter, with a documented/monitored water-budget impact—not on the stated “FV handles it separately” rationale.

- **MAJOR — “mirrors FV” is materially overstated.** FV applies a fourth-order hyperdiffusion, `∇⁴`, not a del2 ([compiled_segments.py:1355](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/compiled_segments.py:1355), [compiled_segments.py:1368](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/compiled_segments.py:1368)). MPAS uses del2 ([model_driver.py:6628](/work/bd1083/b309178/diffESM/legoesm_pg/legoESM/packages/coupler/legoesm/driver/model_driver.py:6628)). Both are unweighted and floored, but del2 damps resolved gradients much more broadly; ∇⁴ is selectively grid-scale. This is a defensible different design, but needs its own scale/physics justification and tuning, rather than parity language.
