  provides a cleaner comparison unaffected by regrid interpol.

What iter-787 DOES measure (observational only):

  (use_duogrid=False) and DUOGRID (use_duogrid=True) paths.
What it does NOT establish:
- WHICH part of the duogrid chain (halo exchange, corner fill,

  finer decomposition than iter-787 provides.
- Whether fixing iter-786's duogrid cube-vertex error would
  make DUOGRID better than LEGACY.
