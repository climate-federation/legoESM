Interpretation cues (observational only):
- NO_MASS_FIX reveals the RAW PPM output.  h_min may be
  negative, mass_drift should be near zero (PPM is inherently
  conservative) but not exactly zero due to truncation.
- CLIP_ONLY keeps positivity but skips the rescale.  If it's
  close to BASELINE, the rescale is cosmetic; if it differs
  much from BASELINE but matches NO_MASS_FIX, the rescale is
  the main post-fix component.

What iter-785 DOES measure:
- Linf, L2, mass drift, h_min / h_max under three variants
  of the `transport_step` post-step correction.
What it does NOT establish:
- Whether the non-Fortran-faithful mass_target fix should be
  removed from production.  FV3 does not apply this post-step
  rescale in `fv_tp_2d`; removal is a fidelity question that
  iter-785 does not itself resolve.
- Whether OTHER parts of the transport step (cross-term, flux-
  form coupling, face-boundary PPM handling) contribute to
  the remaining error.
