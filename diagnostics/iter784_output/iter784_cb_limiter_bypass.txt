Interpretation cues (observational only):
- If NO_LIMITER Linf is MUCH SMALLER (>=30% reduction):
  candidate (d) 'PPM limiter' is supported as a primary
  driver of the cosine bell distortion.
- If NO_LIMITER Linf is similar or larger: the limiter is
  NOT the primary driver.  Candidate (a) 'resolution-
  invariant structural error' becomes more likely.
- NO_LIMITER h_min / h_max may go below 0 / above H0=1000.
  The mass_target fix then re-clips negatives to zero and
  rescales, so the reported Linf is on the re-clipped field.
- The peak_err sign (+/- signs before Linf) distinguishes
  over- from undershoot; BASELINE typically undershoots (-)
  because the limiter clips the PPM parabola.

What iter-784 DOES measure (observational only):
- Linf and L2 of the C36 1-day cosine bell with and without
  the `_pert_ppm_iv0` + face-boundary `_pert_ppm` limiters.
- Shift in peak-error location and sign.
What it does NOT establish:
- Whether a different limiter (e.g. hord=8 2*dm monotone vs
  the current hord=9 positive-definite) would outperform the
  current choice; iter-784 only tests OFF vs ON.
- Whether other components (PPM reconstruction, flux
  integration) contribute independently of the limiter.
- Whether the limiter bypass preserves long-term stability
  (mass_target fix restores non-negativity, but bell shape
  may be unphysical).
