## A. Defects that survive the whole chain (Q1)

**1. BLOCKER — the strategy *mandates* an unfaithful rare branch, and no fixture can exercise it.** §9 prescribes that `_rezone`'s "exceptional no-bracket path must be an explicit returned error flag, a precondition gate, or `checkify` — never a plausible number," with fixtures limited to exact-interface and ±1 ULP. But the NumPy lane transcribed a no-bracket path (`fv3_native_mapz.py:594-669`), which means the *oracle has defined behavior there* — FV remap fallbacks assign clamped/nearest-layer values when `pe` falls outside the column, which happens in nonconvergent NH acoustic iterations and near-topography compression. The prescription therefore violates R1 and the shadow doctrine on precisely the branch where the oracle's robustness matters. Every gate is green (no fixture enters the branch; JAX-vs-NumPy equality is untested there by construction), and production dies at the first rare column — a crashed 40-day run or a masked cell where Fortran produced a physical value. **Change:** transcribe the oracle's fallback literally with `file:line` cited; add a fixture forced into the branch from an instrumented Fortran run (deliberately mismatched `pe`); keep the loud failure as an opt-in diagnostic mode. If the team believes the Fortran fallback masks nonconvergence, report it as a finding — a shadow does not unilaterally "improve" the oracle, which is your own §1 argument.

**2. MAJOR — a conservative-but-wrong seam flux passes tier 2c as written.** The antisymmetry row tests *uniqueness* (flux_A == flux_B at the shared edge), not *correctness* of the blended value. A wrong orientation/sign constant in the shared-edge rotation, applied symmetrically by the same code on both sides, yields a single-valued, exactly conservative flux carrying the wrong transported vector component: a persistent grid-imprinted momentum source at one seam. Kernel gates pass (both lanes identical), dry-mass and per-tracer invariants pass (single-valued flux *is* conservative), and the W2 α=0 envelope gate is a scalar `max|v|` against an envelope with `--tol-factor 1.5` — one bad edge out of twelve can hide inside that margin. That the barriers were "cleared by name" in review makes this worse, not better: review-clearance is one-time and non-executable. **Change:** in 2c, recompute the shared-edge blended flux independently from both panels' *pre-exchange* raw fields and assert the lane's blend equals the independent recomputation; report tier-5 seam norms per edge (12), not aggregated; break the duo/plain 8.2×/59× ratio control out per edge.

**3. MAJOR — hop A has no multi-step certificate, so the "inherited" 1.19e-9 residual has an unmeasured growth rate.** The quoted N-sweep (9.79e-6 / 1.42e-5 / 9.56e-5 at N=1/3/10) *predates* the `fill_corners` fix that produced 1.1866e-9 — the post-fix NumPy-vs-Fortran floor is known only at N=1, while the pinned tree holds unused `96min`/`320min` oracle runs. 1.19e-9 is four-plus decades above fp64 roundoff: that is a real, unidentified mechanism, and §10 waives it while every tier-3 amplification allowance and every tier-5a judgement is calibrated on its N=1 value. **Change:** before Phase 2, run NumPy-vs-oracle parity at 96min/320min and fit the growth; "inherited residual" must carry a measured growth exponent, or hop A reopens for this one item.

## C. Tolerance philosophy (Q3)

**5. MAJOR — bounds pinned to one measurement are pinned to a compiler, a backend, and a state.** `measured × N` freezes current XLA FMA behavior on one CPU; the lane's stated purpose is GPU, where contraction, reduction order, and libdevice transcendentals differ — the census will trip wholesale, or worse, sit just under bounds that no longer certify anything. And the taxonomy keys tolerance to the *kernel*, but conditioning keys to the *state*: `del6_vt_flux` is a 1e-12-class kernel on a rough field and a 1e-6-class kernel on a smooth one; κ=7.78e12 was one worst cell of one state. **Change:** per-output error model `bound ≈ C_ops · κ_est(state) · eps · margin`, with κ estimated per fixture and the smooth/constant state explicitly in the matrix (it is the del-6 operator's *target* regime); a per-backend measurement census; tier 2 run on every supported backend before any bound is cited as certification.

**6. MAJOR — the cell-count gate conflates prevalence with magnitude and misfires both ways.** A defect spreading 1e-7 over 300 cells stays under "2 of 342" and passes; legitimate noise whose κ rises as the flow smooths trips it falsely, and raising the count is threshold tuning. **Change:** ground-truth the instrument instead of counting: in the test, recompute the del-6 stencil with compensated summation (two-sum/Kahan — the *instrument* may be non-literal even though the lane may not) and bound each lane's distance-to-truth. Keep the count only as a secondary tripwire, with the constant-field case (κ→∞) pre-registered.

**7. BLOCKER — no chaos null anywhere; tier 3 and tier 5 have unquantified floors.** "Declared allowed amplification per N" is a hand-declared Lyapunov exponent. At day 5–9 the JW oracle's rms(p_s) growth to 2.38 hPa is dominated by the instability, so a 0.05 hPa lane difference is undiagnosable without knowing the roundoff-equivalent spread — and "field-by-field, pinned" is otherwise pinned to noise. **Change:** build the null explicitly: run the NumPy lane (and the oracle where cheap) with ICs perturbed at 1 ULP and at 1.19e-9, N ∈ {1, 10, 10³}, and record per-field, per-day ensemble spread. Every tier-3 allowance and tier-5 criterion is then stated as a multiple of that null; differences inside the null are labeled UNINFORMATIVE, not passing. This converts tier 5a's "at or below the floor" from a slogan into a distributional test.

## D. Missing entirely for Phases 2–3 (Q4)

**8. BLOCKER — the differentiability deliverable is unrunnable and unmeasured: no adjoint memory policy, no performance gate, no target hardware.** Reverse mode through `k_split × n_split × NSPLT_MAX` stores every substep intermediate; a 3-D NH step at C48 with many tracers under `NSPLT_MAX`-padded subcycling (an unbudgeted NSPLT_MAX× cost multiplier) will not fit device memory, and no document decides checkpointing (`jax.checkpoint` per step? per substep? binomial for rollouts?). "f64 end to end" is a 1/32–1/64 throughput decision on most GPUs, and no device is named. **Change:** add §7b — chosen checkpointing scheme, an asserted memory-ceiling gate per configuration, a throughput floor vs the NumPy lane and the oracle, a named device list — and make "gradient through a 10-step NH integration within memory" a Phase-3 exit criterion.

**9. MAJOR — no inventory of damping/filtering mechanisms, which is exactly where R5 silent no-ops live.** Del-6 divergence damping got attention only because a fixture tripped on it. Nothing enumerates what else the resolved decks activate: background divergence damping on winds, Rayleigh damping on `w` near the model top, any sponge. A missing Rayleigh damping is a silent no-op whose signature is a slow model-top blowup that day-9 tier 5 may or may not isolate. **Change:** a table of every namelist-activated damping/filter in every tier-5 deck — mechanism, resolved value, ported `file:line`, one-step dump coverage yes/no; any "no" gets a dump before Phase 3 exits, plus a standing model-top KE/`w` norm at tier 4.

**10. MAJOR — tier 2c lacks the invariants that catch cross-field compensation: total-energy closure, tracer positivity, cross-field `pe` consistency.** Adiabatic FV3 conserves total energy to truncation; per-field mass invariants are blind to a remap that feeds `delp`, `pt`, and tracers from inconsistent `pe` instances (heating without mass change — thermodynamically wrong, identical in both lanes, invisible field-by-field). Tracer negativity is production-fatal the day moist physics attaches and is where monotone-remap bugs surface first. **Change:** add rows — total-energy budget, `min(q) ≥ 0` after every remap/subcycle, and an assertion that all remaps in a step consumed the identical `pe` instance.

**11. MAJOR — no run-to-run determinism gate.** XLA GPU reductions/scatters can reassociate nondeterministically between runs; tier-3 replay, bisection, and the live twin all *presuppose* run-to-run identity, and a lane whose day-5 state depends on scheduler luck is uncertifiable in practice. **Change:** tier-2 addition — two consecutive jitted runs, same process/device, bitwise equal; cross-process equality documented per backend before any tier-5 number is quoted from it.

**12. MINOR — nesting is named and never scoped.** `divergence_corner_nest` sits in R3's public list and `neststruct%nested` is in the `bounded_domain` definition, yet no port status, fixture, or gate exists. Declare it in §10 (out of scope) or as a phase; ambiguity here is how "port only the calls that appear" defects begin.

## E. Retraction rate (Q5)

**13. MAJOR (process) — the five retractions share one shape: mechanism inferred from symptom, refuted by a sub-minute measurement; the lessons so far are instance-level patches.** Three structural changes: **(a) falsifier-first claim ledger** — a CONFIRMED code-defect claim cannot be committed unless the entry contains two competing mechanisms, the cheapest discriminating measurement, and its raw output; anything less is PROVISIONAL until a second session reproduces it. **(b) Positive controls for instruments**, generalizing the one non-vacuity control you already used (`d_sw1_duo`'s `jnp.where`): every comparison helper must be shown to fail on an injected defect of the class it exists to catch — this alone would have caught `_cmp`'s 0.0-on-non-finite immediately. **(c) Raw-before-interpretation logging** — measurement output committed before any mechanism narrative, so narratives are auditable against numbers rather than substituting for them.
